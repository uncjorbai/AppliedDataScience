# AFES: Algorithmic Fairness Evaluation Standard

**Course:** DATA 740: Governance, Bias, and Ethics in Data Science and AI · **Type:** Governance capstone

A governance capstone proposing AFES (Algorithmic Fairness Evaluation Standard), a structured
framework for measuring, auditing, and enforcing fairness in AI-driven credit scoring.

## The problem

Machine learning models now make credit decisions in seconds across thousands of variables,
but no standardized federal benchmark exists to test whether those decisions are equitable
across demographic groups. Without a common metric, regulators audit inconsistently, lenders
comply selectively, and denied applicants have no meaningful basis to challenge an outcome.
AFES proposes that missing standard.

## What's here

- `final_project.pdf`: the full case study. Role and sector, problem, stakeholders and key
  decisions, ethical matrix, data needs (selection, access, cleaning, curation, privacy), and
  the governance framework
- `nist_framework_mapping.xlsx`: mapping to the NIST AI Risk Management Framework
- `ethical_matrix.xlsx`: stakeholder ethical matrix

## Why it pairs with the ML work

AFES is the governance layer over the same fairness problem tackled technically in
[project 06](../06-machine-learning-final/): disparate impact, statistical parity, and
ratio-based debiasing. Policy framework on one side, measurement on the other.
